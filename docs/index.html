How it plays

One round, four moves.

Everyone starts with 5,000 points. Hit zero and you’re out. When fewer than four players are left, the most points wins.

  1. 1Join a room

    Share a 4-letter code. Friends join from their own phones, or the host fills empty seats with AI players.

  2. 2Bet or sit out

    You get 4 cards. Beat the highest bet (or go all in) to enter, or sit the round out for free.

  3. 3Cut a deal

    The highest bettor is the boss. Everyone else can offer to step aside for a cut of the boss’s winnings. Accept, reject, bluff.

  4. 4Reveal

    The boss reveals, and everyone without a deal reveals too. The best hand takes it.

The rules, short

Hands, best to worst.

The deck uses only the top ranks, one per player. Four players play with A, K, Q and J.
