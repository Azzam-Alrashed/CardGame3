# مداقش (CardGame3) — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

مداقش is a card game you play with friends. We collect as little as possible.

## What we collect
- **An anonymous account ID.** The app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. We never ask for your email, phone number, or real identity.
- **The display name you type.** Other players in your room see it at the table.
- **Game data.** Rooms, bets, deals, and cards are stored on our server (Google Firebase, Middle East region) so the game can run.

## What we don't do
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
- We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

## How long we keep it
Game rooms are kept only to run and show the game. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing the address below.

## Children
The game uses points, never real money, and is not directed at children under 13.
